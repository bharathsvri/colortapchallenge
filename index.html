Color Tap Challenge — Privacy Policy

Android app package: com.b47tech.colortapchallenge

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how B47 Tech (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the mobile application Color Tap Challenge (“the App”). By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

Summary: The App stores game settings and high scores on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and related Google services may process advertising-related data according to your consent choices and Google’s policies. We do not operate a gameplay login or backend server for this App.

1. Developer & contact

Developer / brand: B47 Tech
App name: Color Tap Challenge

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Android app preferences (DataStore):

  • High score
  • Sound on/off preference
  • Vibration on/off preference

This information stays on your device unless you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. It is not uploaded to a B47 Tech server because the App does not use a gameplay backend, account system, or cloud-save feature.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ad formats may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional and are displayed only when you choose to watch an ad, for example to continue a game or receive an in-game benefit.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's policies, AdMob and Google's advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and application technical information
  • Advertising interaction and delivery data
  • Diagnostics related to advertising services

We do not receive your name, email address, or contacts through normal gameplay. Advertising-related data may be handled by Google and its partners according to their respective privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Information

4. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform — UMP)

Where required by applicable law or Google's requirements, including certain regions such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

  • A consent message may be displayed when you first open the App or when consent needs to be refreshed.
  • Advertising requests are made according to the consent status provided by Google UMP.
  • Where required, you may be provided with an option to review or update your privacy and advertising choices.
  • You may also manage advertising personalization through your Android device or Google Account settings where available.

5. App permissions

The App may use the following Android permissions:

  • Internet / network access — required to load advertisements and consent information.
  • Vibration — used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS messages, phone calls, photos, or broad device storage for its core gameplay.

6. Children

Color Tap Challenge is intended as a casual game for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through B47 Tech servers because the App does not provide user accounts or operate a gameplay backend.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at bharathsvri@gmail.com .

7. Data sharing

Local gameplay preferences and high scores are not shared by B47 Tech through a backend server.

Advertising-related data may be processed or shared by Google AdMob and its advertising partners as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

B47 Tech does not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention

  • On-device data: retained on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising-related data: retention is governed by Google AdMob and applicable advertising partners according to their policies.

9. Your choices

  • Turn sound or vibration on or off in the App settings.
  • Manage advertising and consent choices through Google's consent interface when available.
  • Manage advertising personalization through your Android device or Google Account settings where supported.
  • Clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove locally stored gameplay preferences and high scores.

10. Security

We use reasonable development practices intended to protect the security and integrity of the App.

Because gameplay settings and high scores are stored primarily on your device, maintaining device security, installing system updates, and downloading applications only from trusted sources can also help protect your information.

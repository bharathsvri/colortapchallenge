2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Android app preferences (DataStore):

  • High score
  • Sound on/off preference
  • Vibration on/off preference

This information stays on your device unless you clear app storage or uninstall the App. It is not uploaded to a B47 Tech server because the App does not use a gameplay backend, account system, or cloud save feature.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ad formats may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional and only shown when you choose to watch an ad (for example, to continue a game).

Depending on your region, device settings, and Google’s policies, AdMob and Google’s advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and app technical information
  • Ad interaction and delivery data
  • Diagnostics related to ad serving

We do not receive your name, email, or contacts through normal gameplay. Advertising data is handled by Google and its partners under their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy (Google Help)

4. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform — UMP)

Where required by law or Google’s requirements (for example, in the EEA, UK, or other regions), the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for advertising and related data processing.

  • A consent message may appear when you first open the App or when required by your region.
  • Ads are requested only when permitted by your consent status.
  • You may be able to update your choices in the App under Settings → Privacy & ad choices when that option is available.
  • You can also manage ad personalization through your device and Google account settings where available.

5. App permissions

The App may request these Android permissions:

  • Internet / network state — to load ads and consent forms
  • Vibrate — optional haptic feedback (can be disabled in Settings)

The App does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, phone, photos, or broad storage access for core gameplay.

6. Children

Color Tap Challenge is intended as a casual game for a general audience. The App is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers (we do not operate gameplay accounts). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the email above.

If you publish the App to audiences that include children, you must comply with applicable laws and Google Play Families policies, including appropriate ad and data settings in AdMob and Play Console.

7. Data sharing

Local gameplay preferences and high scores are not shared by B47 Tech through a backend server. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google AdMob and its partners as described in Section 3. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention

  • On-device data: kept until you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data: retention is governed by Google / AdMob and their partners.

9. Your choices

  • Manage ad/consent choices via UMP (when shown) and App Settings.
  • Reset advertising ID or limit ad personalization in Android / Google account settings.
